About the Event

Saints 2027

Prioritizing sustainable research is essential to tackle global challenges like climate change and resource depletion. Fostering innovation that balances economic, social, and environmental needs is needed to bolster long-term well-being for all. Advancing scientific research focused on sustainability creates a resilient and harmonious relationship between human activities and the planet, leading to a healthier and more equitable world.

In this regard, CHRIST (Deemed to be University) is happy to welcome you to the international conference on Scientific Advances In Natural and Technological Sciences (SAiNTS - 2026) providing an excellent platform for experts and researchers to share knowledge and exchange ideas so as to understand and address the challenges that lie ahead for a sustainable future.

About the Institute

CHRIST (Deemed to be University)

Founded in 1969, CHRIST (Deemed to be University), Bengaluru, is one of India's leading higher education institutions, known for its commitment to academic excellence, research, innovation, and holistic education. The University offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across sciences, engineering, technology, humanities, social sciences, management, law, commerce, and education.

CHRIST has consistently been recognized among the top institutions in India for its quality education, research contributions, and global engagement. The University is accredited with NAAC A++ Grade, features prominently in the NIRF Rankings, and has established a strong reputation for fostering interdisciplinary research and industry collaboration.

With a vibrant research ecosystem comprising specialized research centers, state-of-the-art laboratories, funded research projects, and collaborations with national and international institutions, CHRIST actively promotes innovation in areas such as energy, environment, advanced materials, artificial intelligence, health sciences, and sustainable technologies.

The University maintains strategic partnerships with numerous universities, research organizations, and industries across the world, facilitating faculty and student exchanges, joint research programs, international conferences, and collaborative innovation initiatives. Through its global outlook and commitment to societal impact, CHRIST continues to contribute significantly to scientific advancement and sustainable development.

About the Deanery

School of Sciences

The School of Sciences at CHRIST (Deemed to be University) is a premier academic and research division dedicated to excellence in scientific education, innovation, and interdisciplinary research. The School offers undergraduate, postgraduate, doctoral, and post-doctoral programs across diverse disciplines, including Chemistry, Physics and Electronics, Mathematics, Life Sciences, Computer Science, Statistics, and Data Science. Research and scholarly activities form an integral component of its academic framework, fostering a strong culture of inquiry and innovation among students and faculty alike.

The School hosts state-of-the-art laboratories, advanced research facilities, and specialized centers that support cutting-edge research in areas such as advanced materials, renewable energy, green hydrogen, nanotechnology, artificial intelligence, data science, biotechnology, astrophysics, and environmental sustainability. Faculty members actively undertake research projects funded by national agencies including DST, SERB, ISRO, BRNS, UGC, and other prestigious organizations.

With a strong emphasis on interdisciplinary collaboration, innovation, and societal impact, the School of Sciences has established extensive national and international research partnerships. Through its vibrant research ecosystem, international collaborations, conferences, publications, and industry interactions, the School continues to contribute significantly to scientific advancement and the development of sustainable technological solutions.
